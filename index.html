GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
jump in.

Still curious? Talk to the Falcon team ↗

Who is Water Tag designed for?

Falcon supplies commercial Water Tag equipment to camps, resorts, outdoor attractions and event operators running group activities.

What do I need to get started?

You’ll need an appropriate outdoor space, access to water, equipment and trained staff. Discuss your venue, field layout and operating requirements with Falcon before launch.

Can I expand my package later?

Yes. Falcon’s packages can grow with additional vests and launchers. Ask the team about equipment quantities and the best layout for your operation.

Is operator support included?

Falcon provides operator guidance covering game formats, field layout and staffing. Confirm the training and support included in your chosen package when requesting a quote.

How do I get pricing and shipping information?

Browse the official packages or contact Falcon with your country, venue type and preferred equipment capacity for a current quote.
